I study matrix time series and high-dimensional statistics, with a focus on latent factor models and their identification.

I am a Research Assistant in Statistics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), working under the supervision of Professor Chun Yip Yau. I received my MPhil in Mathematics from CUHK.

Research interests

My research focuses on quasi-maximum likelihood estimation (QMLE) under non-affine identification structures, and the associated challenges in high-dimensional statistics.
